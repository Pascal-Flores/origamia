# Exercice 11 — ligne-quatre-traits

Outil : imagegen intégré. Illustration seule sur fond transparent, assemblée avec compose_thumbnail.py et layout.json.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: isolated illustration for an Origamia exercise thumbnail. Create ONLY the subject described below, on a genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Consistent series style: very simple friendly flat 2D educational pictogram, rounded clear shapes, clean solid color fills, minimal necessary details, legible at 96 pixels. Strictly use ONLY the specified brand palette colors; no extra colors, gradients, grain, texture, reflections, highlights, 3D effects, drop shadows, heavy cartoon outlines or unnecessary ornaments. Do not generate the thumbnail background, white disk, badge, framing circle or scenery. Center the complete uncut subject with modest transparent margins on a square canvas, including transparent openings. No text unless explicitly requested in the subject. Subject: A horizontal DASHED LINE made of EXACTLY FOUR separate identical short horizontal straight dashes. Four navy #2f3366 thick short strokes with slightly rounded ends, equal size, equal gaps, all on the same horizontal axis: dash gap dash gap dash gap dash. No fifth mark, no dots, no border, no arrows, no other objects. Use a wide horizontal arrangement centered in the canvas. The four separate dashes must be clearly countable. This is the whole requested illustration.
