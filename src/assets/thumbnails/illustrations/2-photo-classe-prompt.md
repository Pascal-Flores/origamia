## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: isolated illustration for an Origamia exercise thumbnail. Create ONLY the subject described below, on a genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Consistent series style: very simple friendly flat 2D educational pictogram, rounded clear shapes, clean solid color fills, minimal necessary details, legible at 96 pixels. Strictly use ONLY the specified brand palette colors; no extra colors, gradients, grain, texture, reflections, highlights, 3D effects, drop shadows, heavy cartoon outlines or unnecessary ornaments. Do not generate the thumbnail background, white disk, badge, framing circle or scenery. Center the complete uncut subject with modest transparent margins on a square canvas, including transparent openings. No text unless explicitly requested in the subject. Subject: A small landscape printed CLASS GROUP PHOTO, depicted as a very simple flat illustration, NOT a photorealistic image. A rectangular blue photo frame containing two compact rows of friendly schoolchildren, simplified rounded heads and shoulders, tiny facial features only. No scenery and no camera. Use ONLY #3f85db blue for frame and clothes, #ffe6a6 cream for faces, and #2f3366 navy for hair and minimal facial details. Three colors are allowed here for recognition. No text.
