# Exercice 7 — crayon

Outil : imagegen intégré. Illustration seule sur fond transparent, assemblée avec compose_thumbnail.py et layout.json.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: isolated illustration for an Origamia exercise thumbnail. Create ONLY the subject described below, on a genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Consistent series style: very simple friendly flat 2D educational pictogram, rounded clear shapes, clean solid color fills, minimal necessary details, legible at 96 pixels. Strictly use ONLY the specified brand palette colors; no extra colors, gradients, grain, texture, reflections, highlights, 3D effects, drop shadows, heavy cartoon outlines or unnecessary ornaments. Do not generate the thumbnail background, white disk, badge, framing circle or scenery. Center the complete uncut subject with modest transparent margins on a square canvas, including transparent openings. No text unless explicitly requested in the subject. Subject: One simple sharpened PENCIL placed diagonally. Clear long straight body and a pointed writing tip. Exactly two colors: #ffa04e orange body and sharpened wood, #9a6f03 deep ochre graphite tip and one optional thin boundary line at the sharpening. No eraser, branding, lettering, shadow or extra objects.
