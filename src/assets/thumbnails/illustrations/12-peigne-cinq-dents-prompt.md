## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: isolated illustration for an Origamia exercise thumbnail. Create ONLY the subject described below, on a genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Consistent series style: very simple friendly flat 2D educational pictogram, rounded clear shapes, clean solid color fills, minimal necessary details, legible at 96 pixels. Strictly use ONLY the specified brand palette colors; no extra colors, gradients, grain, texture, reflections, highlights, 3D effects, drop shadows, heavy cartoon outlines or unnecessary ornaments. Do not generate the thumbnail background, white disk, badge, framing circle or scenery. Center the complete uncut subject with modest transparent margins on a square canvas, including transparent openings. No text unless explicitly requested in the subject. Subject: One simple COMB with EXACTLY FIVE broad clearly separated teeth. A horizontal rounded spine at the top connects exactly five equal vertical teeth pointing downward. Exactly four equally wide transparent gaps between the five teeth. All five teeth identical length and thickness, aligned. No handle extending down and no extra side prongs that could be counted as teeth. Flat turquoise #68cfcd with at most a fine dark teal #417f7c outline. Do not add texture or decorative lines. The five teeth must be immediately countable at thumbnail size.
